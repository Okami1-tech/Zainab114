A letter from Hamzain
💌

My Sarauniya

Written with love.
Sealed with a little du'a.

My Sarauniya, 💜

When you told me, "I wish I can be close to you," I wished for the same thing.

I wished distance could become a little smaller. I wished I could be there to ask how you're feeling, bring you something warm, help you get comfortable, and make the evening a little easier for you.

Not because I think I can make every discomfort disappear, but because sometimes caring for someone means wanting to be present even when there is nothing extraordinary you can do.

I know that for now, some of our care has to travel through messages and words. So I want my words to carry something real: patience, reassurance, kindness, and the intention to keep learning how to love you well.

My Queen, you don't need to be perfectly okay before you talk to me. You don't have to hide the small things until they become big things. I want you to feel safe telling me when something is bothering you, without feeling that you have to explain everything perfectly.

And I want to remember that caring for you also means respecting when you need quiet, rest, or a little space. Love should feel gentle, not like another thing you have to manage.

I pray Allah teaches me how to be the kind of man whose presence brings comfort, whose words are trustworthy, and whose actions make his love easier to believe.

Until we can share more of these ordinary moments in person, please take good care of yourself for me—and, most importantly, for yourself.

Tonight, get some rest, my baby girl. You don't have to finish this little world all at once. It will still be here when you wake.

With love and sincere du'a,
Your Hamzain 🦁💜
Chapter V · A prayer for my Queen

What I ask
Allah for you.

☾

Ya Allah, You know what is hidden in our hearts and what we struggle to put into words.

Please grant my Sarauniya shifaa, ease her discomfort, restore her strength, and bless her with peaceful rest. Protect her wherever she is and surround her with Your mercy.

🌷 ✨ 🪻

Ya Rabb, teach me to care for her with patience, gentleness, honesty and responsibility. Correct my shortcomings and help me become a man whose actions reflect the goodness he asks You for.

Let our affection bring us closer to You, and grant us what is best in this life and the Hereafter.

🤍 🌙 🤍
Ameen, Ya Rabb. 🤲🏻
